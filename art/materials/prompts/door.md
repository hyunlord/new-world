# Door texture prompt template, schema version 1

Create ONE 64×60 game door component, {style_description}, made of {material}, era
{era}, in the specified OPEN or CLOSED state. Match the attached wall's construction
scale, ink contour and earth-colour washes. Upper-left lighting. Full standing
silhouette in fixed wall height, transparent outside, no ground shadow or background.
OPEN has transparent aperture and visible frame; CLOSED fills the aperture with the
actual slab. No label, text, perspective vanishing point, modern hardware or scenery.
