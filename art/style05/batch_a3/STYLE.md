# A3 item production rules — 2026-11-10

Source: `/Users/rexxa/work/art-batch/BRIEF.md`, content bible issue 690,
C2 GOAL, ADR 0018 and `art/style05/PROJECTION.md`.
This art-only branch supplies candidate adoptions for later C2/C3 import.

- Match the adopted ENV1/POLISH items and concept-05 props. Hand-ink natural-history
  illustration with a restrained translucent pigment wash, never pixel art or 3D.
- Warm brown-black contour; main/secondary line width ratio **2:1**. At the 64 px
  export, main marks aim at 0.8–1.2 px, secondary marks 0.4–0.6 px (antialiased).
- Hatch direction is screen **lower-left to upper-right**, in small shadow groups.
  One material face has at most 3 groups of 2–3 parallel marks. Wood uses 3–5 grain
  lines; stone/ore 3–5 facet seams and 2 hatch groups; metal 2–3 edge accents and
  1–2 hatch groups; clay 2–3 irregular marks; cloth/fibre 3–5 folds plus 2 short
  weave groups; food 2–4 identity marks. No all-over stippling or white speckles.
  These are visual design targets; do not claim exact automated mark counts.
- Quiet earth palette: ochre, umber, cream, muted mineral green, clay red.
  Copper is subdued reddish brown, bronze muted yellow ochre, tin pale cool grey,
  iron dark neutral grey. No metallic glare or neon colours.
- One front three-quarter projection, about 20 degrees down; upper-left light.
  Objects rest naturally, showing a narrow top face. No ground or cast shadow.
- Single isolated item per generation. Transparent alpha; no labels, grid, border,
  decorative scenery, watermark, floating particles, or cropped parts.
- Final canvas 64×64, source alpha preserved through crop/uniform Lanczos fit;
  declared icon anchor (32,32), footprint (1,1), maximum 58 px occupied extent,
  matching the actually imported ENV1/POLISH item family. The STRUCT 4–24 px
  map-item band is explicitly provisional and is not this inventory-icon contract.
  Initial small map exports remain external diagnostic evidence only.
- Count buckets 1 / 2–8 / 9+ use the existing ENV1 count-layout composition
  (1 / 3 / 9 whole-item copies); ordinal density, not literal count.
  Count variants only where a pile is sensible; unique equipment remains single.
- Paleolithic/neolithic: no metal or dyed cloth. The newer brief puts wool, linen,
  dyed cloth and mud brick in bronze; flag older bible/data-era disagreements in
  the import draft rather than silently relabelling integration data.
- Preserve every prompt, source, timestamp, tool hint and raw SHA externally in
  A3. Seed is null because the built-in image tool does not expose one.
- Existing style/projection instruments are retained without changing thresholds.
  Numeric signals and visual decisions remain distinct; record every rejection.
- Compare native 64 px and half-scale 32 px contact sheets alongside old items;
  each visual iteration gets a structured verdict before a repair generation.
- Inspect actual alpha-composited previews on neutral and green grounds. The
  generation tool's inline preview can expose RGB hidden by zero alpha and show
  a false brown aura. A maximum alpha of 254 does not itself indicate a defect.

Production order: bronze metal chain; remaining bronze items; neolithic gaps;
paleolithic gaps; iron; explicit state and count variants; coverage audit.
