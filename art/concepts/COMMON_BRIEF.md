# ART-CONCEPT — Common brief

The concepts differ in **style**; they must not differ in **content or camera**, or the comparison is unfair.
- **Camera (amended 2026-10-22, TRIAL2b):** ground and buildings remain straight top-down with a slight front-face cheat for tall objects. Map characters use front three-quarter view from about 20° above: visible face/chest, head about 30% of height, wide shoulders, simplified limbs, mitten hands, wedge feet, clear hair and tunic shapes, and a generated 2-px dark contour readable at 48px. South is front, east side three-quarter, west flipped east; north optional. Feet touch the tile's lower third with a soft contact shadow; axe head is head-sized, shoulder logs wider than the body. This replaces the overhead-character rule. The S1–S8 table below records the historical concept comparison, not current character-production instructions.
- **Content:** stone age. Hide, bark, logs, reeds, river stones, thatch, bone, ochre, charcoal. No metal, no dyed cloth, no modern anything. A small founding camp by a river at a forest edge.
- **Mood baseline:** the game's words are 촘촘함 (dense), 흙과 돌 (earth and stone), 화덕 불빛 (hearth light). A concept may interpret them its own way (warm or cold, soft or hard), but every scene has a hearth as its warmest point and people must read against the ground.
- **No text, no watermark, no UI** in any image. Magenta background only for the two cut-out subjects (§4.7); all others are full scenes or objects on their natural ground.
