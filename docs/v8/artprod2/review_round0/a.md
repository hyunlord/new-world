# Independent blind visual review A — round0

Inspected all 48 PNG contact sheets with view_image detail=original: 12 IDs, both native scales, sequence1 then sequence2 for every ID/scale. Only the round0 README, manifest, and images were read. No key, source, sibling packet, or other review was consulted.

24 judgments are in a.json. Confidence is a subjective 0–1 overall reading confidence, not a correctness score. Unknown labels preserve uncertainty; no label-frequency assumptions were used.

Raw observations: walking and work read as conspicuous alternating poses, rather than smooth motion, in these sampled sequences. The axe nearly disappears in its lowered pose. Bundled logs are clear but merge with the hair silhouette and hide head/shoulder cues. Static standing and lying sequences show no visible pose change. Sleeping collapses garment and sex-presentation cues; the dark sleeping covering is a tentative cloak reading. Small child bodies are legible, but their sex presentation is not independently clear. Native 2x improves outline separation without resolving these ambiguities. Both grounds retain usable silhouette contrast.

No pass/fail threshold or answer-key score was inferred.
