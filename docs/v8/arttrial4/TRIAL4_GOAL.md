## Why
Six trials settled the environment (concept 05, judge v2, 35/38) and never produced a usable person. Route A (2D generation inside guides) gave crouched grey figures; route B (free 7-bone rig) gave peg dolls; the owner saw both on 2026-10-22 (`compare_AB_2x.png`) and rejected them at a glance while the numeric judges reported ~2.5/5. The common failure is the brief's demand for **limbs, hands, feet and joints at 56 px**. RimWorld reads perfectly at that size with none of them: a head, a body capsule, hair, apparel, a held item, three facings, a bob. Consistency comes from construction, not from a model's goodwill. This trial builds that: every pawn is drawn from a declarative spec, so attribute-driven variation (skin, hair, top, item, sex, age) is free and the layer contract holds by definition. Portraits are not in scope — close-ups are what generation does well (TRIAL2) and stay generated.

# 2. PART 1 — The pawn generator (`art/pipeline4/pawn_gen.py` + `pawn_spec.py`)
- **Spec, not code:** `pawn_spec.py` holds data only — shapes as lists of primitives (ellipse, capsule, polygon, polyline stroke) in 1× template coordinates with anchors, per part and facing; `pawn_gen.py` rasterizes at 4× and downsamples with Lanczos. Everything a designer or artist might change lives in the spec.
- **Look table `PawnLook`:** `sex {M,F}`, `age {child, adult, elder}`, `skin_tone 0–3`, `hair_style 0–5` (none, short, long tied, bun, braid, grey-thin for elder), `hair_color 0–3`, `top {none, hide_wrap, fur_cloak, woven_tunic}`, `top_palette 0–1`, `item {none, stone_axe, log_bundle, basket}`, `facing {S,E,N}`, `state {idle, walk_a, walk_b, work_a, work_b, carry, sleep}`. Palette values come from `art/style05/palette_05.json` (add entries if missing, documented). `art/character/PAWN_ATTRIBUTES.md`: the table mapping these fields to the sim's entity attributes (Identity sex, Age band, Body/skin, equipment → item/top) — a mapping document only; wiring is M-ART2.
- **Frames:** idle = base; walk_a/b = body and head bob −1/+1 px with item sway ±4°; work_a/b = item rotated −30° / +40° about the grip point, body lean 2 px toward the item; carry = log bundle across the shoulders at y = 56, wider than the body; sleep = rotated layout. The body silhouette is identical across frames except the bob and lean (fixed-upper-body check applies to overlays).
- **Outputs:** per layer PNG RGBA at 1× plus mask PNG, sidecar JSON (look fields, anchors, spec hash), in the layer-contract folder layout the lab already reads (`art/character/route_p/…`). Generate the **full adult set**: 2 sex × 4 skin × 6 hair × 4 hair colours (hair only) × 4 tops × 2 palettes × 4 items × 3 facings × 7 states — as **layers**, not combinations: body layers = 2 sex × 4 skin × 3 facings × 7 states (168) plus child/elder bodies (droppable), hair 6 × 4 × 3 (72), tops 4 × 2 × 3 (24), items 4 × 3 × 3 states (36). Generation is deterministic (same spec → same bytes; test it).

# 3. PART 2 — The ink pass and the checker
- **Ink pass (`art/pipeline4/ink.py`), applied at 4× before downsampling, same rules as the environment's code companion (TRIAL2b §4.3) made explicit:** outline 8 px at 4× (2 px at 1×) in `#141210` from the alpha edge; interior strokes from the spec (hair strands ≤ 4, cloth folds ≤ 3, 4 px at 4×); 3-band flat shading — base, shadow band −18 % luminance on the lower-right 35 % of each shape (light from upper-left), highlight +10 % on the upper-left 15 %; paper grain 8 % multiplicative noise seeded by the spec hash; no gradients, no quantization. Parameters live in `ink_params.json` (counts as spec for the revision limit).
- Run the checker on every layer and `check_set.py` on 48 sampled combinations (same sampler as TRIAL3): expected 100 %. A failure here is a generator bug — fix the generator, rerun (does not count as a spec revision unless the spec changes).
- Judge v2 (person family) on 6 stills and the model judge's four scores: **reported, not a gate** (the anchors are generated illustrations; a code-drawn pawn may legitimately score differently). Your own one-line reading per still.
- Boards: `art/character/boards/pawn_sheet_{1x,2x}.png` (all tops × hair for one body, S/E/N), `pawn_states_{1x,2x}.png` (7 states × 3 facings), and `compare_ABP_2x.png` (the TRIAL3b board's 6 A/B pairs with the pawn equivalent as a third column, **on the calm meadow from `art/style05/env/`**, not the busy forest floor).
