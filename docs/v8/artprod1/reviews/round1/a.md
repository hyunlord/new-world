# Blind visual review A

Diagnostic observation only; no scoring or certification.

Inspected 48 PNG contact sheets directly with view_image: both chronological sequences for all 12 IDs at each of 1x and 2x. Read scales separately. Only packet README, manifest and the 48 PNGs were read. No answer key, code or previous reviews were consulted.

Visual problems:

- p1_08 has no visible character in either facing, sequence or scale.
- p1_05 has one missing facing. The remaining curled sleeper obscures clothing and sex; age and hair are especially unclear at 1x.
- Walking, carrying and working show abrupt switches between held poses. p1_03 loses its axe in the standing pose before showing it overhead.
- Shoulder logs obscure hair and facial details; small carriers do not give a confident sex presentation.
- Small pale/brown garment edges merge with skin at 1x. The diagonal wrap on p1_11 is clearer at 2x.
- Side-lying sleep silhouettes make sex presentation unclear even where activity is clear.

Confidence is an overall qualitative reading, not a probability or accuracy score. Unknowns are intentional and remain in the JSON; notes preserve uncertain observations. Dataset eligibility was not used to infer character attributes.
