## 2. The obs2 split (Part 1)

**Why.** WM-4's observational split had one act per world, 400 train worlds and IPW effective sample sizes of 44–55: a
reviewer could say balancing had nothing to work with. obs2 has three times the worlds (1,200 train, 200 val, 240 test),
a **sequential multi-act policy** (up to five acts per world, ≥ 8 days apart, each decision read from the trajectory
already changed by the earlier acts), two reference rows per evaluation world and two noise replicates per test family.

**The policy** (METRICS.md part a): WM-4's frozen terciles and every daily probability doubled (gift 0.060 / 0.016 /
0.004, spoil 0.004 / 0.016 / 0.060 for the food-per-person terciles low / middle / high; rot 0.024 / 0.008 by the wood
tercile), its own random stream, a refractory week after each act, at most five acts.

**Recording in rounds.** Round 0 recorded the 1,640 factual worlds; round k re-recorded every world that acted in round
k − 1 with all its acts so far, and the policy continued from the row after its last act. Rounds 1–5 recorded 1,587 /
1,318 / 757 / 265 / 39 worlds; round 6 found no new act. Every round's episodes equal the previous round's on every row
up to the new act (5 × all OK) and carry every earlier act; replaying the policy on a world's final episode reproduces
every round's acts and row probabilities (1,640 / 1,640), so the propensity of every decision row is known.

**Realized.** 3,966 acts, **2.42 per world**, **3.2 % of worlds never acted** (GOAL.md expected 2–3 and ≈ 3 %); 39.9 %
gift, 39.9 % spoil, 20.1 % rot; **60.0 % of acts had an earlier act in the world's history**. The confounding is WM-4's:
in the low-food tercile 80.9 % of acts are gifts and 5.3 % spoils; in the high-food tercile 4.3 % gifts and 72.9 %
spoils. **IPW effective sample sizes**: 396–445 treated per act at 7 days and 126–141 at 30 days (WM-4: 44–55), from
1,163 / 1,174 / 572 treated units at 7 days, more than half of them with an earlier act.

**Families.** For every val / test world at c1 (first act, or day 30) and c2 (second act, or c1 + 14 days): `pre` (the
acts before c) and the three arms (`pre` + the act at c), forked at c; test families with two replicates. 880
families, 7,080 manifest episodes; `fam-check` 880 / 880 (every member equals the observed episode on rows < c and
carries exactly `pre` + its act; `none_r0` = the round-0 factual in all 880; `pre_r0` = a fresh unforked recording in 3
sampled test families). The first `fam-check` failed on a checker bug (DECISIONS L1.5). obs2 test: 240 worlds × 2
references × 3 acts = 1,440 primary cases (replicate 0) and their 1,440 replicate-1 twins for the floor.

**What the test asks.** The truth shows WM-2's 30-day reversal again: a gift's food effect is +21.7 at 7 days and −17.3
at 30; a spoil's −78.0 and +32.8; per-world spread is large (gift 7-day std 41.2).

## 3. CRN and CT as their authors train them (Parts 2 and 4)

Each stage trains for the authors' number of optimizer steps (CRN 15,700 + 53,800, CT 11,850), α follows the authors'
schedule on real progress, the **last** checkpoint is primary (CT's EMA weights) and counts are rounded at readout as for
M1 (METHODS.md D16–D20); the lowest-validation checkpoint and the unrounded readout are secondary. Cost checks (300 s on
the actual data) never reduced a plan: CRN-v2 0.097 / 0.040 s per encoder / decoder step (3,694 s predicted), CT-v2
0.998 s (11,998 s), CRN-obs2 0.044 / 0.011 s (1,275 s), CT-obs2 0.910 s (11,006 s; measured a third time, DECISIONS
L4.4: the first two ran beside our own evaluations).

## 8. Q4 — how much of WM-4's CRN / CT deficit was rounding? (D2)

**Almost none.** Re-scoring WM-4's eight kept CRN / CT runs with M1's readout rounding on their own test splits changes
the seed-averaged 12-cell scores by −0.006 to +0.018, every interval containing 0 (obs: CRN +0.001 [−0.007, +0.010] at
7 d, −0.003 [−0.008, +0.002] at 30 d; CT −0.006 [−0.014, +0.003], −0.004 [−0.010, +0.003]; v2: CRN +0.003 [−0.085,
+0.099], −0.004 [−0.029, +0.021]; CT +0.018 [−0.060, +0.098], +0.016 [−0.013, +0.043]). The deaths cells barely move
(WM-4's runs, e.g. `wm4:ct_v2_s0` 7 d 1.409 → 1.549, `wm4:crn_v2_s0` 30 d 4.187 → 4.183): the two rollouts of CRN / CT differ by whole deaths,
not by fractions that rounding would remove. WM-4's unrounded predictions are reproduced exactly by the re-score's
unrounded readout for seven runs; `ct_obs_s1` differs at the 1e-4 relative level (GPU nondeterminism, DECISIONS L4.2).
