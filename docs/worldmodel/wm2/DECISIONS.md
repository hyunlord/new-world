## Part 1
- **H1.1 Two manifests.** `v2_manifest.json` lists the `game_v19` families and the `game_v16` control and is written before the first training (its sha256 goes into every run's `config.json`); the noise-floor families (6.4) are recorded beside the first training and get their own `v2_floor_manifest.json` (same entry format), so the training manifest never changes after a run has read it. Both are built by `wm2/families_v2.py manifest` (`--part main|floor`), which verifies every episode (`wm_data.verify_episode`), asserts every cast tick is a multiple of 60, checks the directory name, scene, fork tick, replicate, `git_sha` = the merge commit, rows / days / every, and that each arm's single intervention matches its kind and requested amount.
- **H1.2 Manifest splits.** `split` encodes the member's place in a family, because `data.load_split` filters on `split` and `role` only: `v19_fact` (replicate 0 of `none`), `v19_arm_r0`, `v19_none_r1`, `v19_arm_r1`, `v16` (the control), `v19_floor_s<s>` / `v16_floor_s<s>`; `schedule_id` = the arm (`none` for factuals). A dataset group may name a tuple of splits (`common.split_names`, `load_groups` concatenates them in order), so `v2_v19`'s training group is every member of the train families and its validation group the replicate-0 members (factual and arms) of the val families, as GOAL.md 7.1 states. Per-world family directories of symlinks (`v2/v19/fam/game_v19_s<seed>/`, `floor_s<s>/fam/…`) serve `wm_data.load_family`.
- **H1.3 Empty schedule directory.** A factual-only fork (6.4) is `--schedule-dir <empty dir> --include-factual --fork-tick s --replicates 4`; checked on a 3-day scratch run (`_scratch_floortest`, seed 90000): four members `none_r0 … none_r3`, and `none_r0`'s `global.npy`, `settlements.npy` and `latent.npy` bit-identical to an unforked recording of the same world.

## Part 2
- **H2.1 Rollout extension.** `wm2/rollout_ext.py` re-implements the two M1 predictors' rollout arithmetic batched over (episode, start, actions) jobs and also returns `g.settlements_alive` and `g.fissions_total`; `evaluate.Rollout`, `m1_gru.GRUPredictor` and `m1_tf.TFPredictor` are not changed (so WM-1.2 / WM-1.3 goldens cannot move), and a test checks that the jobs reproduce `pred.rollout` on V, fire, cold hours and deaths for the TF, the GRU with window warm-up and the GRU with full burn-in, rounded and unrounded. The two extra series are the continuous stepped state, never rounded (the split event is defined on it); `round_counts` applies to V, cold hours and deaths as in the predictors. Batching is needed for speed: a test family needs up to 12 rollouts of up to 2,620 steps.
- **H2.2 Last-row day.** By `timeline_p14.py`'s formula the last row (2,880, tick 172,800) is 08:00 of display day 121; GOAL.md 7 calls it "day 120" (the episode's length). The restricted split day uses display days throughout, so the censoring day is 121 (`v2.LAST_DAY`), for truth and prediction alike; a split shift between two censored branches is 0 either way.
- **H2.3 Validation report group.** `m1_gru.train` writes full validation metrics (T1, T2, T2-any, bias-removed) at the end of every run; on 400 validation episodes this would cost ≈ 30 min per run (WM-1.3: ≈ 370 s for 80). `common.Dataset.val_report` names the groups of that report: for the v2 datasets the factual validation worlds (`val_fact`); early stopping and the rounding choice stay on the `val` group (GOAL.md 7.6). Every older dataset has no `val_report` and keeps its behaviour.
- **H2.4 Validation-start subsample.** `--val-frac F` (both model kinds, default 1 = every start, WM-1.3) scores early stopping and the rounding choice on a fixed subsample of the validation 06:00 starts: per episode in group order, ceil(F × n) of its n starts by one `default_rng(0)` stream (`m1_gru.val_starts`), the same for every run on the same validation set. The fraction is set from the cost check (GOAL.md 7.6) and recorded in Part 4.
- **H2.5 Full burn-in (M1-long).** `m1_gru` gains `burn_in` ∈ {`window`, `full`}. Training with `full`: `data.sample_windows(..., return_starts=True)` returns the episode indices and window starts of the same draws; `m1_gru.burn_in_states` runs rows 0 … start − 1 of every window's episode through `proj` and each layer's `nn.GRU` in one no-grad pass, the different lengths packed with `pack_padded_sequence` (zero-length burn-ins get a zero state); the window then runs with gradient from those per-layer states (truncated BPTT), and the rollout loss continues from the window's states as before. Evaluation with `full`: the teacher-forced states of every row are computed once per episode (one `model.run` over the whole episode) and indexed by start; tested equal to a warm-up of start + 1 rows from a zero state, and the window batch / losses of `window` tested bit-identical to WM-1.3's code on a fixed seed.
- **H2.6 Effect-baseline details.** `an5_arm` uses the factual train worlds' split std (`bench_v0.split_std` of replicate 0 of `none`) as `an5`'s scale; for each lag it averages the 5 nearest candidates that have that lag (a cast late enough for c + 30 days to pass the last row has no 30-day value), at most one per world (the nearer replicate; ties by world seed, then replicate); the shift uses the 5 nearest of all candidates. `arm_mean` is the train mean at each lag. The baseline for W3 / W4 is chosen once on the validation families (both replicates) by the same cell average as the rule.
- **H2.7 Split-timing explanation.** "How much of each predictor's error θ explains" is computed after every test prediction exists: leave-one-out R² of a ridge (λ from `bench_v0.LAMBDAS` by the LOO error) of the predictor's signed error (prediction − truth) on (a) the window summary (mean of the 8 V over the 24 window rows) and (b) θ plus the summary; θ's share = (b) − (a); θ alone and corr(θ, error) are reported beside it. θ = the home settlement's (lowest id) `fission_threshold` at the start row.

## Part 4
- **H4.1 Batched validation instead of a start subsample.** The first cost check (`runs/cost/cost_{tf,gru}`, code `f3ded074`, every validation start, per-episode rollouts as in WM-1.3) gave M1b-act 1.459 s/step at k = 168 and a validation pass of 15.5 s (7.1 % of the 150 steps between evaluations), M1-long (`--burn-in full`) 0.368 s/step and 23.5 s (43 %). The M1-long pass is dominated by per-episode work (386 episodes, each a full teacher-forced pass and a 24-step rollout), so a subsample of starts would have had to drop to ≈ 2 % of the starts to get under 10 %. Instead the validation pass rolls every (episode, start) pair through `wm2.rollout_ext.rollout_jobs` in batches of 512 (`--val-batch 512`; the same rollouts, tested equal to the per-episode path within 1e-5 on both kinds and both burn-ins, `test_wm2.BatchedValidationTests`). Re-check (`runs/cost/cost_batched2_*`, code `a34c8396`): M1b-act 1.554 s/step, validation 7.8 s (3.6 %); M1-long 0.396 s/step, validation 1.6 s (2.7 %); both under 10 %, so GOAL.md 7.6's subsample is not triggered (`val_frac` 1: every validation start; the validation scores at step 200 were the same to four decimals as the per-episode path: 0.0964 and 0.1075). GPU memory: M1b-act ≈ 21–23 GB, M1-long ≈ 2 GB.
- **H4.2 Run lengths.** M1b-act and the control: `--max-steps 12000` (≈ 5.2 h at 1.55 s/step plus ≈ 80 validation passes of 8 s). M1-long: `--max-steps` = min(12,000, ⌊0.95 × 21,600 / 0.407⌋) = 12,000 (0.407 s/step = 0.396 + 1.6 / 150). Every run gets `--max-seconds` = min(21,600, deadline − now − 3,600) from `queue.sh`, which re-reads `_setup/deadline_epoch` (2026-10-16 23:41:40 KST plus recorded pauses) before each run; no run starts with less than 2 h.
