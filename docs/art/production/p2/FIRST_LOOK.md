# Part 2 first windowed look

`d2_1200_F2` was inspected from the actual 1920×1080 Godot framebuffer after the native Sprite2D installation. Villagers now retain their D3 body proportions and dark silhouettes without state tint or scale pulsing, and buildings overlap them through one feet-anchored y-sorted layer. The old ground remains excessively busy and square-edged, while HUD text lacks panel backing and provisional builder/elder sprites exceed the size budget; Parts3,4 and6 address these remaining issues.
