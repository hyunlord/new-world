Compute the union of intersecting rectangles with strictly greater draw rank; exclude coverage above50%. Record every exclusion before creating an image. If short, record the missing kinds and add the same seed/day at11:00 and14:00 centered on the lowest-AgentId holder of that kind.

The preserved P10 crop is square, ceil(max(body width,body height)+16) screen pixels, centered on the body, displayed at4x nearest-neighbor. It is not enlarged to include the overhead icon. Offscreen bodies or crops extending outside the screenshot are recorded as excluded.
