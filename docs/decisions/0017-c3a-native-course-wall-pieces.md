# C3A Pass2: one native course field for stone wall topology

## Context

BRIEF4 explicitly approves a second pass for stone walls and three additional
anvil attempts. Pass1 mixed a sharp stone face with a broad blurred cap. The
pinned shipped log pieces at visual-integ d086d7c54 use26px thickness,16px caps
and22px fronts. The original W2 composer lives in external art-import-data/tools,
while Git holds its committed outputs; both sources were inspected read-only.

## Decision

Reuse the accepted drystone face field at literal native scale. Select and
record one64x32 crop, repeat it without filtering, and compose W2 geometry with
that single field for all stone surfaces. Keep source crop scores and nonzero
edge discontinuities explicit; independent actual1x/2x5x4 andL-room inspection,
next to pinned logs, decides whether tile joins are visible. Reuse the exact
shipped wooden EW/NS open/closed doors. Corner/end caps alias the matching
masks. Deliver art and additive capability proposals, with no runtime edits.

The anvil alone gains a cumulative six-attempt budget. Try4 passes unchanged
style and projection plus independent alpha-matte review. Existing opaque-bound
normalization is unchanged. Preserve Pass1 evidence and keep CompositeBow held.

## Alternatives considered

- Enlarge or blur the old cap: rejected because it caused the visual failure.
- Paint individual complete walls: rejected because they lose16-mask topology.
- Generate another texture before testing the existing sharp native field:
  rejected because accepted source pixels already provide the required density.
- Treat a numerical seam statistic as proof of invisible joins: rejected;
  actual room comparison is required and wrap differences remain recorded.

## Consequences

Pass1 wall candidates stay held as historical evidence. New Pass2 stone art
and the anvil are independently accepted; game import and mixed-material,
chunk/cache/state-selector integration still require the integration pass.
The existing workshop lifecycle previews retain their old wall-kit source;
replace that source through the new capability rather than flattening previews.
