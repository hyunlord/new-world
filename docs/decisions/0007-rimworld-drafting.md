# ADR 0007 — RimWorld-style drafting

## Context
In a settlement sim the player normally does not control individuals, but threats (animals, raids) need a direct response.

## Decision
Drafting is allowed: only drafted people receive direct move/attack orders; undrafting returns them to their normal work. No direct control otherwise.

## Alternatives considered
No direct control at all (rejected: no answer to threats); full direct control (rejected: breaks autonomy).

## Consequences
Drafting arrives with the threat pressure in M5 (animal threats).
