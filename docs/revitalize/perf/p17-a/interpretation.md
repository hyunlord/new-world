**What the recorded system timers support.** Median `AgentDecisionSystem` time per call rises from **233.182 to 342.657 µs** (+46.95%); its median share rises from 66.02% to 72.91%. The 109.475 µs difference is about 94% of the 116.694 µs difference between whole-tick medians. This is a descriptive comparison of medians, not an exact additive causal decomposition. Settlement cost stays near 67.901/68.367 µs; movement is 4.267/4.018 µs. Memory increases 18.716/20.825 µs and stale-target cleanup 1.770/2.554 µs. The principal observed regression is therefore within decision-system execution, rather than movement or a large settlement-system increase.

The tail pattern repeats: v21's three p99.9 values are 2.646, 2.530 and 2.582 ms. All repeats have their largest tick at **170 (10:50)**, then **282 (12:42)**, then **370 (14:10)**. At tick 170, decision time is 2.867–3.039 ms of 3.028–3.207 ms total; at 282 it is 2.396–2.532 ms of 2.530–2.646 ms total. Decision is the largest recorded system in all 20 retained slowest ticks of every run, including v20. Periodic systems are due at some peaks, but their own recorded times do not dominate. Repeated tick locations support a simulation-state-dependent workload; they do not identify the responsible routine operation. Each p99.9 rests on very few tail samples, so these are repeat observations, not a confidence interval.

**Source-based hypotheses, not profiler proof.** The following work occurs inside the measured decision system; the summaries contain no separate substep timers or replay counts:

- **Per-tick reconstruction:** [RoutineTick::new](https://github.com/hyunlord/new-world/blob/50a73474e06d3a9abbb535729e9c7e1e134954b7/rust/crates/sim-systems/src/runtime/decision/routine/mod.rs#L55) scans Activity/rest/agent/lineage components, sorts active rows and settlements, and rebuilds several ordered maps. This runs whenever the rule is present, including outside the entry window. [Place collection](https://github.com/hyunlord/new-world/blob/50a73474e06d3a9abbb535729e9c7e1e134954b7/rust/crates/sim-systems/src/runtime/decision/routine/places.rs#L32) also rebuilds candidates, examining up to 29×29 tiles per settlement plus building ownership/ring checks. These are plausible sustained overheads, not measured attribution.
- **Activity preemption checks:** [the pre-match predicates](https://github.com/hyunlord/new-world/blob/50a73474e06d3a9abbb535729e9c7e1e134954b7/rust/crates/sim-systems/src/runtime/decision/agent_decision.rs#L1181) perform full forage/firewood/gather availability searches for activity holders. Eligible selectors scan resource maps. The separate local calculations occur before the combined exit condition; an interrupted holder then enters the ordinary Idle cascade. This can add searches or repeat work. Actual qualifying holder/search counts were not recorded here.
- **Starts, redraws and lifecycle bursts:** [start](https://github.com/hyunlord/new-world/blob/50a73474e06d3a9abbb535729e9c7e1e134954b7/rust/crates/sim-systems/src/runtime/decision/routine/mod.rs#L148) scans candidate lists with occupancy lookups; a failed initial choice checks all seven kinds for the redraw. [Lifecycle application](https://github.com/hyunlord/new-world/blob/50a73474e06d3a9abbb535729e9c7e1e134954b7/rust/crates/sim-systems/src/runtime/decision/routine/lifecycle.rs#L8) sorts pending operations, collects observation events and inserts/removes ECS components. Cohorts transitioning together could create peaks. No event-to-timing join establishes that they explain ticks 170 or 282.
- **Selected-peer replay:** [the replay loop](https://github.com/hyunlord/new-world/blob/50a73474e06d3a9abbb535729e9c7e1e134954b7/rust/crates/sim-systems/src/runtime/decision/agent_decision.rs#L1073) recollects the full mutable-query row vector before retaining invited IDs for another pass. Its cost depends on invitations and retry waves; the data does not prove replay occurred at the peaks. The ordinary first-pass AgentId sort already exists in keyed v20, so it cannot simply be charged as wholly new sorting work.

**Limits on the conclusion.** The three-size fit supports approximately proportional decision cost over the measured 100/500/1,000-agent, two-day cases; three points do not prove asymptotic complexity or bounded tails in other worlds. The v19 quick control is close (mean 0.304749/0.303219 ms; p99.9 0.597/0.609 ms), supporting the absence of a broad control slowdown, not cancelling the valid v21 failure. Routine-related state changes can also alter work performed by existing decision branches. These summaries cannot distinguish direct helper overhead, changed behavior, allocation/archetype effects and replay. R5 remains an observed mean-and-tail budget failure while Stage A evaluation proceeds under the existing gates.
